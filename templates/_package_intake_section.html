What these terms mean

Received package
Files physically received and preserved by ContractIQ.
Initial package
The first set of customer files for this Bid.
Addendum
A later customer release that adds, changes or withdraws Bid information.
Incorporated
Reviewed and deliberately included in the Bid Basis.
Current Bid basis
The exact set of customer releases and documents on which the offer is being prepared.
Expected or missing addendum
A customer release known to exist but not yet received.
